Section E
Preliminary Key Findings
Patterns emerging from early responses. Will be updated as sample size grows.
📉
Score compression in AI pipeline
Our pipeline scores cluster in 0.37–0.64, making Hybrid the dominant prediction. Combined with LLM scores (50/50 weighted average) improves classification accuracy to ~67%.
🔄
Complementary pipeline errors
Our pipeline underscores Unconventional items; the LLM overshoots Unconventional for Hybrid items. Combining both signals outperforms either alone.
🎯
Hybrid class hardest to detect
The 50/50 combined model achieves 100% recall for Unconventional but only 18% for Hybrid — the H/U boundary is the primary classification challenge.
⚖️
Optimal weighting: 50/50
Across tested product categories (table lamps, desk clocks), equal weighting achieves the highest combined accuracy at 67% against human ground truth.
