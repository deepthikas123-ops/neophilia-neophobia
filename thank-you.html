📋 What this study is really about
This study examines neophilia (attraction to novelty) and neophobia (aversion to novelty) in product design across generational groups — Gen X, Gen Y, Gen Z, and Gen Alpha. The products you evaluated were pre-classified using an AI pipeline developed at IISc Bangalore that scores each design on a scale from 0.00 (most conventional) to 1.00 (most unconventional). Your choices will help us understand where the "Neophilia-to-Neophobia shift point" lies across different generations, genders, and income groups.
